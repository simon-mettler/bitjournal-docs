# Install as an app (PWA)

bitjournal is a **Progressive Web App (PWA)**. That means it is a website that you can install on your phone's home screen and open like a normal app, without an app store.

## What a PWA is

A PWA is a regular website with a few extras that let your device treat it like an app:

- It gets its own **icon** on your home screen or app list.
- It opens in its **own window** without the browser's address bar and tabs, so it feels like a native app.
- It **updates without a store**. When a new version is available, the app tells you and updates with one tap.

## Installing

Open your bitjournal instance in your phone's browser, install it as described below, then open the installed app and log in there.

:::info
Menu names vary a little between browsers and versions. If you can't find the option, look for "Install" or "Add to Home Screen" in your browser's menu.
:::

### iOS/iPhone (Safari)

1. Tap the **Share** button.
2. Choose **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Tap **Add**.

### Android (Chrome)

1. Tap the **⋮** menu.
2. Choose **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
3. Confirm.

## Updating and uninstalling

The app updates itself. It checks for a new version and installs it automatically at a safe moment. To check manually, open **App** settings and tap **Check for updates**. To remove it, uninstall it like any other app on your device. This only removes the app, not your account or data.

:::info
**For self-hosters:**
The app is delivered by your own server, so it can only update itself after you have updated your bitjournal Docker setup. See [Updating](../selfhost/updating.md).
:::

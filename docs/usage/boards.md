# Boards

The **Log** screen shows your signals as a grid of cards.

- **All** is always present and shows every signal.
- Your own boards show only the signals you put on them, in the order you chose.

## Editing boards

Press the pencil icon at the top of the Log screen to enter edit mode. Press the check mark when you are done.

In edit mode you can:

- **Add, rename, delete and reorder boards** using the tabs.
- **Add signals** to the selected board with the *Add signal* tile. You can pick existing signals or create a new one.
- **Reorder signals** by dragging the handle on a card.
- **Remove a signal** from the board with the ✕ on its card. This only removes it from the board; the signal and its data are kept.

## Signal menu

Outside of edit mode, each card has a menu with:

- **View stats**: opens the statistics of the signal.
- **Edit signal**: opens the signal's settings.

# Getting started

bitjournal is a flexible personal journal for tracking anything you care about: coffee, mood, workouts, sleep, symptoms, weight or whatever else you can count, rate, measure or time. You define your own **signals**, log them as they happen, and look back at them later as a timeline. Built-in analytics turn your data into numbers and charts, so you can spot trends and patterns.

## Quickstart

1. [Create an account](/pricing) or check the [self-hosting guide](../selfhost/overview.md) if you want to run your own instance of bitjournal.
2. [install bitjournal as an app on your phone](./install-app.md) and then log in with your credentials.
3. [Create a signal](./signals.md). If you have none yet, the log screen shows a hint that guides you.
4. Go to the **Log** screen, tap the signal and press **Save Entry**. See [Logging events](./logging.md).
5. Open the **Journal** to see your entry.
6. Add a widget to your **Dashboard** to see it as a number or a chart. See [Dashboard](./dashboard.md).

New to the terms? Read [Concepts](./concepts.md) first.

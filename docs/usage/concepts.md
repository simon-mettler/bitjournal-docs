# Concepts

bitjournal has a small number of building blocks.

## Signal

A [signal](./signals.md) is one thing you track, for example "Coffee", "Mood", "Weight" or "Run". Signals are what makes bitjournal flexible: you decide what to track and how. A signal can be something you count (coffees, headaches), rate on a scale (mood from 1 to 5), measure with a unit (weight in kg) or time (a run, a meditation).

## Board

A [board](./boards.md) is an ordered collection of signals that you use for quick logging, for example "Morning" or "Health". A signal can be on several boards. The **All** tab always shows every signal.

Boards are only a convenience for logging. They don't change your data.

## Event

An [event](./logging.md#logging-an-event) is one moment in time. It has a date and time and contains one or more signals, each with its value.

So "coffee and a mood of 4 at 08:15" is one event with two signals. Logging several signals together lets you see what happened at the same time.

## Dashboard and widgets

The [dashboard](./dashboard.md) is where you analyse your data. It is made of [widgets](./widgets/index.mdx) that you arrange yourself. A widget turns the data of a signal into something you can read at a glance. Use them to keep an eye on what matters to you and to spot trends and patterns.

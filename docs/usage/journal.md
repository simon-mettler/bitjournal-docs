# Journal

The **Journal** is a timeline of everything you have logged. Events are grouped by day, newest first. Each card shows the time and a chip for every signal with its value.

The menu on a card lets you **Edit event** or **Delete event**. Deleting asks for confirmation and cannot be undone.

## Filters

Open the **Filters** panel to narrow the list:

- **Date boundary**: show only events up to and including a date.
- **Signals**: choose one or more signals with _Add signal_. Choose **Any (OR)** to show events that contain at least one of them, or **All (AND)** to show only events that contain every one of them.

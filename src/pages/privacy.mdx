# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026.11.19_

This policy explains how personal data is processed when you visit this website or use the hosted bitjournal service. It follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

If you self-host bitjournal, you are the controller of your own installation and this policy does not apply to it.

## 2. Data we process

**Website visits.** Our hosting provider may record technical data such as IP address, date and time, requested page and browser type in server logs. This is needed to deliver and secure the site. The website does not use cookies for tracking or advertising.

**Account data.** When you register: email address, password (stored hashed), and account settings.

**Journal content.** The signals, boards and events you create and log in the service. Depending on what you track (for example mood, sleep or symptoms), this may include **health data**, which is sensitive personal data. We process it only to provide the service to you, and only on the basis of your explicit consent. We ask for it on a separate screen right after you register, before you can use the service, and record when you gave it. You can withdraw consent at any time with one click in your account settings; this deletes your account and all your data. We do not use your journal content for advertising, profiling or analytics, and we do not sell it.

**Transactional emails.** We send emails needed for the service, such as email verification and password reset, and a warning before an inactive account is deleted (see section 6).

**Payment data.** Payments and subscriptions are handled by Paddle (see section 4). We do not receive your card or bank details.

**Support.** If you contact us, we process your message and email address to answer you.

We do not share your data with other third parties unless required by law.

## 5. Data transfers

Your journal content is stored on servers in the EU. Switzerland and the EU recognise each other's data protection as adequate. Where a provider processes data in a country without an adequacy decision (for example the US), we rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. 

## 6. Retention

- Account and journal data: until you delete your account or withdraw your consent, which deletes them.
- Accounts without an active subscription (you cancelled, or you registered but never started one): kept for 90 days so you can subscribe again and pick up where you left off. We email you at least 14 days before the deletion. After that the account and all its data are permanently deleted. If you subscribe again before then, nothing is deleted.
- Failed payments: your access continues while our payment provider retries the payment (about 30 days). If the payment is not recovered, the subscription is cancelled and the 90 days above start.
- Server logs: 90 days.
- Billing records: 10 years, as required by Swiss accounting and tax legislation.

## 7. Your rights

You can request access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, a copy in a portable format, and object to processing. You can withdraw consent at any time. Write to contact@simonmettler.com.

You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: in Switzerland the [Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC)](https://www.edoeb.admin.ch), or the authority in your EU country of residence.

## 8. Security

We use encryption in transit and appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. We will notify registered users of material changes.

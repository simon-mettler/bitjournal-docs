# Refund policy

_Last updated: 2026.11.18_

This policy applies to paid subscriptions to the hosted bitjournal service. Self-hosting the open-source software is free and not covered by it.

## 1. Who processes your payment

Orders are processed by our payment provider **Paddle**, which acts as the seller (merchant of record). Refunds are issued by Paddle on our behalf, to your original payment method. Paddle's [buyer terms](https://www.paddle.com/legal/checkout-buyer-terms) also apply.

## 2. 14-day refund

If you are not satisfied, you can request a full refund within 14 days of your first purchase of a subscription, without giving a reason.

## 3. Renewals

Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. If you were charged for a renewal and did not intend to continue, contact us within 14 days of the charge and we will refund it, provided you have not meaningfully used the service in the new billing period.

## 4. Cancelling

You can cancel at any time. Cancelling stops future charges; access continues until the end of the period you already paid for. Cancelling does not by itself trigger a refund for the current period.

## 5. Service discontinued

If we discontinue the service, we will refund any prepaid period you have not used (see the [terms of service](/terms)).

## 6. Statutory rights

This policy does not limit any statutory rights you have as a consumer, such as mandatory withdrawal rights under the law of your country of residence.

## 7. How to request a refund

Email contact@simonmettler.com from the address on your account and include your order or receipt number if you have it. You can also request a refund directly through Paddle using the link in your receipt. Approved refunds are usually paid out within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.
